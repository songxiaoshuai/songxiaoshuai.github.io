Biography

I am a first-year Ph.D. student of RUC-NLPIR Lab in the Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence, Renmin University of China (RUC), advised by Prof. Zhicheng Dou and Prof. Ji-Rong Wen. Previously, I received my M.S. degree from the School of Artificial Intelligence, Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications in June 2025, advised by Prof. Weiran Xu.

My current research interests center on Large Language Model (LLM) agents, specifically focusing on (1) Web Information Acquisition, and (2) General Tool Use.
